My Current Work

I am currently the Head of Actuarial at Breach Insurance, an insurtech start-up focused on building regulated insurance solutions for the crypto and web3 industry. We develop proprietary technology and risk management tools for retail and institutional clients holding digital assets.

There are currently critical coverage gaps in traditional cyber policies, where digital asset losses are typically excluded. By combining modern technology, deep industry expertise, and a commitment to fair responsible underwriting and pricing, we deliver regulated insurance solutions that give individuals and institutions the confidence to participate in the evolving digital asset economy.

My Expertise

I'm a modern actuary with strong technical skills. I combine deep domain knowledge with modern tools and techniques to deliver value for the whole enterprise.

My core skills include:

Ratemaking & Reserving

Crunching complex data to price risks accurately and building reliable models to forecast future claims are at the core of my work. I also design business specific diagnostic tools to detect early signs of model deviation to enable the leadership team to make informed decisions on course correction.

Capital Management

I perform stress tests and extreme scenario modeling to optimize an entity's capital allocation, keeping it both robust and efficient. This work blends financial engineering and risk management, all while making sure we satisfy the ever-changing complex regulatory standards.

Data Engineering

I am proficient in modern programming languages, such as Python and SQL. I build scalable data pipelines, and deploy models that automatically transform raw data into beautiful visuals that are easy to understand. I bring full-stack capabilities to actuarial work, combining data science and engineering expertise.

Although my full time role keeps me more than busy, I'm always open to connecting with professionals who'd like to bounce ideas around on new actuarial problems, data modeling, or complex risk challenges. These conversations keep me sharp and plugged into emerging industry issues.
